Tools

Everything here talks to the device over USB from your browser (Chrome or Edge on a computer). Plug it in and press one of its buttons first. Nothing is uploaded to the internet.

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. You can still make and download badges below.

Badge maker

Drop in an image, tweak it, then send it to the device (it opens straight away) or download a 200×200 1-bit BMP.

Plug the device in and press one of its buttons to wake it, then Send.

Clock & calendar

Set the device's clock to this computer's time.

Send the next 7 days of a calendar to the Timetable. Export a calendar file (.ics) from Apple Calendar (File → Export), Google Calendar (Settings → Import & export) or Outlook, and drop it here. Repeating events are included. Sending also sets the clock.

Drop a .ics file here, or click to choose

    Each send replaces the events on the device. If you also use the automatic Mac sync, the Mac sends its own calendar the next time the device is plugged in.

    Automatic calendar sync (Mac)

    A small background app on your Mac sends the time and your Apple Calendar (next 7 days, all calendars) every time the device is plugged in and awake. It needs the Xcode Command Line Tools (xcode-select --install).

    git clone https://github.com/Forrest404/unidex.git
unidex/tools/calsync/install.sh

    Allow Calendar access when macOS asks. Remove it again with unidex/tools/calsync/install.sh uninstall.
