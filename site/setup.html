Setup

The WiFi networks your device joins, and the keys for voice notes.

What you type here goes over USB to your device and nowhere else. This page can't reach the internet. Privacy (draft)

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Plug the device in and press one of its buttons to wake it, then Connect.

WiFi & keys

Leave a password or key blank to keep the one that's saved. The Test buttons run on the device itself, over its own WiFi.

WiFi networks

You can save up to five. The device joins whichever is in range, starting with the one that worked last time.

    Add a network

    Be careful with this password. The device can't tell the real network from a fake one with the same name, and a fake one could capture it.

    • Only use it on networks you trust.
    • Don't use a password you use anywhere else.
    • Adding your university's CA certificate above makes it safer.

    (It logs in with PEAP / MSCHAPv2.)

    The device checks that the network's certificate was signed by your CA.

    That only stops a fake network if your university uses its own private CA, because the device can't check the server's name. If the CA is a public one, treat the network as unchecked.

    OpenAI

    Make one at platform.openai.com/api-keys. Billing has to be turned on. Whisper costs about $0.006 a minute. Not needed if ElevenLabs transcribes and Claude (or nothing) tidies up.

    Transcription

    Uses the OpenAI key above.

    Make one at elevenlabs.io/app/settings/api-keys (a key with every permission is simplest; a restricted one needs Speech to Text, and User for the Test button). The free plan's 10,000 credits a month are about half an hour of notes, with no card needed, for personal use.

    Free setup: ElevenLabs here and the tidy-up off below. Notes then keep the plain transcript with a short title, and no OpenAI account is needed.

    Tidy-up
