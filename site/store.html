Apps

Small apps for your unidex, written by anyone and checked before they are listed. They live on the SD card and open from Apps on the device. Plug it in, press one of its buttons, then Connect.

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer to install apps. You can still look through them below.

    Make your own

    An app is a folder with a short app.json and a main.lua. The guide has the whole API, the limits, and an example to start from: writing apps for unidex. Apps are sent in as pull requests to unidex-apps and checked before they are listed; submissions are not open yet.
