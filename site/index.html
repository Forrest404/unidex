Install unidex

A small e-ink OS for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54: timetable with calendar events, name badges, a WiFi Pokédex and a chooser. Install it straight from your browser, with nothing to download.

  1. Plug the device into this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Press one of its buttons so it's awake.
  3. Click a button below and pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit from the list.

Install

A fresh start: the firmware plus three starter badges. Erases everything on the device.

Your browser can't do this. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.

Update

The newest firmware only. Keeps your badges, Dex, calendar events and settings.

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.

Nothing in the list? The device is asleep: press a button and try again. Still nothing (a brand-new board, or one with other firmware)? Unplug it, hold BOOT while plugging it back in, let go, then try again; press a button after it's installed.

After installing

Set the clock and send your calendar or badges from Tools. On the device: A = next, B = select, hold A = back home (or Settings, on the home screen), hold A + B for a second = restart.
