Install unidex

Put unidex on a board, or update the one you have. Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. No board? Try it in your browser.

  1. Plug the device into this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Press one of its buttons so it's awake.
  3. Click a button below and pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit from the list.

Install

Starts from scratch. It erases the device's settings, including your WiFi and the keys saved for Notes.

The files on your SD card aren't touched, and your Pet's friends come back from there.

Beta has the newest changes before they're released, so it may have bugs. You can go back to Release with Update.

Your browser can't do this. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.

Update

Gets the newest version of unidex and keeps everything else: your badges, apps, calendar, notes, settings, keys and Pet.

Beta has the newest changes before they're released, so it may have bugs. You can go back to Release the same way.

Nothing in the list?

  • The device is probably asleep. Press a button and try again.
  • If the board is brand new, or has other software on it, unplug it. Hold BOOT while you plug it back in, let go, and try again. Press a button once it's installed.

After installing

unidex keeps your files on a micro SD card in the device's slot. The card needs to be formatted as FAT32. To fill it:

  • Send badges, the time and your calendar from Tools.
  • Or copy the starter files ↗ onto the card yourself. They're a badges folder and an empty timetable.csv.
  • For voice notes, add your WiFi and keys on Setup.

The buttons, the battery and resetting the device are on Help.
