Install unidex

A small e-ink OS for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54: timetable with calendar events, voice notes, name badges, a WiFi Pokédex and a chooser. Install it straight from your browser, with nothing to download.

  1. Plug the device into this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Press one of its buttons so it's awake.
  3. Click a button below and pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit from the list.

Install

A fresh start: erases the device's firmware and settings, including the WiFi and keys saved for Notes. Your files live on the micro SD card and aren't touched.

Your browser can't do this. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.

Update

The newest firmware only. Keeps your badges, Dex, calendar events, notes, settings and keys.

Nothing in the list? The device is asleep: press a button and try again. Still nothing (a brand-new board, or one with other firmware)? Unplug it, hold BOOT while plugging it back in, let go, then try again; press a button after it's installed.

After installing

Files live on a micro SD card (FAT32) in the device's slot. Send badges, the clock and your calendar from Tools, or copy the starter files (a badges folder and timetable.csv) to the card. For voice notes, set up WiFi and your keys on Notes. On the device: A = next, B = select, hold A = back home (or Settings, on the home screen), hold A + B for a second = restart.
