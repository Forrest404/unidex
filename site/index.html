Install unidex

unidex is a small operating system for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54 e-ink board. You get a timetable that shows your calendar, voice notes, a Pet you dress up and take to meet your friends' Pets, name badges, one-button games, a game where you collect WiFi networks, and a chooser for when you can't decide. It installs from this page, so there's nothing to download.

Don't have a board? Pre-orders for a ready-made unidex, assembled and tested, have closed, but you can join the wait list.

  1. Plug the device into this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Press one of its buttons so it's awake.
  3. Click a button below and pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit from the list.

Install

Starts from scratch. It erases the firmware and settings on the device, including your WiFi and the keys saved for Notes. Your files are on the micro SD card, so they aren't touched, and your Pet's friends come back from there.

Your browser can't do this. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.

Update

Puts the newest firmware on and keeps everything else: badges, Dex, calendar events, notes, settings, keys and your Pet.

Nothing in the list? The device is probably asleep, so press a button and try again. If it's a brand-new board or has other firmware on it, unplug it, hold BOOT while you plug it back in, let go and try again. Press a button once it's installed.

After installing

The device keeps your files on a micro SD card (FAT32) in its slot. You can send badges, the time and your calendar from Tools, or copy the starter files (a badges folder and timetable.csv) onto the card yourself. Voice notes need WiFi and your keys, which you add on Setup.

On the device, A moves to the next thing and B selects. Hold A goes back, and hold B does whatever extra the screen offers. Every screen shows what its buttons do. Hold A and B together for a second to restart. The first time it starts, it shows you the buttons and a QR code that links here.

When the battery is low, anything that needs a lot of power waits and says "Battery too low". When it's flat, it shows "Charge me" and switches off. Plug it in, then press PWR. Giving it to someone else? Settings > Reset data > Factory reset wipes the WiFi, keys, Pet and settings. Format the SD card as well.
