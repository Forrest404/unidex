Install unidex

unidex is the software for a small e-ink gadget, the Waveshare ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54 board. You install it from this page, with nothing to download.

What it does:

  • A timetable that shows your calendar
  • Voice notes
  • A Pet you dress up and take to meet your friends' Pets
  • Name badges
  • One-button games, and a game where you collect WiFi networks
  • A chooser for when you can't decide

Don't have a board? Pre-orders for a ready-made unidex, assembled and tested, have closed, but you can join the wait list.

  1. Plug the device into this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Press one of its buttons so it's awake.
  3. Click a button below and pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit from the list.

Install

Starts from scratch. It erases the device's settings, including your WiFi and the keys saved for Notes.

The files on your SD card aren't touched, and your Pet's friends come back from there.

Your browser can't do this. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.

Update

Gets the newest version of unidex and keeps everything else: your badges, Dex, calendar, notes, settings, keys and Pet.

Nothing in the list?

  • The device is probably asleep. Press a button and try again.
  • If the board is brand new, or has other software on it, unplug it. Hold BOOT while you plug it back in, let go, and try again. Press a button once it's installed.

After installing

Your files

unidex keeps your files on a micro SD card in the device's slot. The card needs to be formatted as FAT32. To fill it:

  • Send badges, the time and your calendar from Tools.
  • Or copy the starter files onto the card yourself. They're a badges folder and an empty timetable.csv.
  • For voice notes, add your WiFi and keys on Setup.

The buttons

ANext
BSelect
Hold ABack
Hold BThe screen's extra action
Hold A and B for a secondRestart

Every screen shows what its buttons do. The first time it starts, it shows you the buttons and a QR code that links here.

The battery

  • When it's low, anything that needs a lot of power waits and says "Battery too low".
  • When it's flat, it shows "Charge me" and switches off. Plug it in, then press PWR.

Giving it to someone else

Go to Settings > Reset data > Factory reset. It wipes the WiFi, keys, Pet and settings. Format the SD card on a computer as well.
