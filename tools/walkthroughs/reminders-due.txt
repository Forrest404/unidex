# Reminders falling due (device only: each one chimes). The sample list (X DEMO 1), with the clock moved to just
# before its times; the clock is put back at the end. Over the open list, then over Home: done, later, left.
demo 1
select Reminders
press b
clock today 07:59:50
sleep 15000
expect screen=Reminders
shot d-alert-over-list
press b
shot d-done-back-to-list
press A
expect screen=Home
clock today 16:59:50
sleep 15000
expect screen=Reminders
shot d-alert-over-home
press a
expect screen=Home
shot d-later-home
clock today 17:09:50
sleep 15000
expect screen=Reminders
shot d-snoozed-alert
press A
expect screen=Home
shot d-left-home
