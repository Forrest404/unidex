# Phone setup from Settings: hold B in the list starts the hotspot (Setup first without WiFi, else Settings), the
# QR screen, a press keeps it up, hold A stops it and, with nothing changed on a phone, goes back to the list. Leaves
# the hotspot off. The page itself needs a phone (or a computer joined to the hotspot: X STATE gives its password).
select Settings
press b
expect screen=Settings
shot pt-list
press B
shot pt-qr
press a
shot pt-qr-press-a
press A
shot pt-stopped
press A
expect screen=Home
